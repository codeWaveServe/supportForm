Privacy Policy


Clear VPN: Secure Fast Proxy


Introduction

At Clear VPN, your privacy and data security are core priorities. This Privacy Policy outlines how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our apps, websites, and related services. The term “personal data” refers to any information that relates to an identified or identifiable individual, and we treat such data in accordance with relevant privacy regulations.

Who We Are

This policy applies to Clear VPN: Secure Fast Proxy. In this document, “we,” “our,” and “us” refer to the Clear VPN service and its managing entity responsible for handling your data.

What We Collect

Information You Provide

  • Account Details: If you create an account, we may collect your name, username, and login credentials.
  • Payment Information: For paid services, we may collect billing names and payment methods.
  • Verification Data: Some features may require identity verification.
  • Support & Communication: Any details you provide via support requests, surveys, or feedback forms.

Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage Data: General metrics about app usage, such as session durations and data consumption.
  • Device Data: Information such as device ID, model, operating system, language, browser type, and network status.
  • Diagnostics: We collect anonymized diagnostics for service performance improvements. We do not log your browsing history or VPN activity.
  • Approximate Location: Used to recommend optimal servers, based on IP address or device settings.

Information from Third Parties

  • Referrals: We may receive limited data if another user invites you to use Clear VPN.
  • Third-Party Sign-In: If you log in using services like Google or Apple, we may access basic profile details.
  • Threat Intelligence: We may use data from trusted sources to protect against malicious activity.

How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, operate, and maintain our services
  • To process payments and manage subscriptions
  • To communicate service-related updates and offers
  • To improve the app’s performance and user experience
  • To develop new features and security enhancements
  • To prevent misuse or fraud
  • To fulfill legal obligations
  • To deliver promotional or marketing content (where legally permitted)

Sharing Your Data

We only share your information when necessary:

  • With your explicit consent (e.g., for third-party sign-in)
  • With trusted service providers bound by confidentiality agreements
  • In business transitions (e.g., mergers or acquisitions)
  • With authorities when required by law or to respond to legal requests
  • To prevent fraud or abuse of the service
  • In anonymized or aggregated form for analytical or research purposes

Cookies & Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to enhance performance and functionality. You can manage cookie preferences via your browser or app settings. Clear VPN does not respond to "Do Not Track" (DNT) signals.

Data Security

We employ industry-standard security measures—such as encryption, access controls, and regular audits—to protect your personal data against unauthorized access, loss, or misuse.

International Transfers

Your data may be stored and processed outside of your home country. We implement safeguards to ensure that data is handled securely and in compliance with applicable laws.

Data Retention

We retain personal data only as long as necessary for the purposes stated in this policy or as required by law. You may request deletion, subject to applicable retention rules.

Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct or delete inaccurate information
  • Restrict or object to certain processing activities
  • Request a portable copy of your data
  • Withdraw consent where applicable

To exercise these rights, contact us through the support section of the Clear VPN app or website.

California Privacy Notice (CCPA)

If you are a California resident, you may:

  • Request details about personal data collected in the last 12 months
  • Request deletion of your personal data
  • Opt out of data "sales" (Note: Clear VPN does not sell user data)
  • Inquire about third-party sharing practices

Clear VPN does not track users across third-party websites and does not respond to DNT signals.

Children’s Privacy

Clear VPN is not intended for users under the age of 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If such data is discovered, it will be deleted promptly.

Policy Updates

We may update this Privacy Policy occasionally to reflect changes in practices or legal requirements. When changes occur, we will update the "Last Updated" date and notify users in-app when appropriate.

Contact Us

For any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please reach out via the support feature in the Clear VPN app or visit our official website.
